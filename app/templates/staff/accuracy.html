How to read these numbers

The dataset is generated by VeriDoc's own tool: fictional candidates on mark sheets that follow the general TN HSC and CBSE XII structure, half of them degraded with blur, skew, low light and noise. The engine was developed against sheets from the same generator, so these results show the method works on these templates. They are not a measurement of accuracy on real mark sheets, which would need a test on real (consented, anonymised) documents.
